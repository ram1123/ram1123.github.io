The CMS paper on inclusive and differential Higgs boson cross sections in the H→4l channel has been published in the <em>Journal of High Energy Physics</em>. I contributed to this analysis by introducing the n-jettiness observable and expanding the EFT interpretation using MadGraph_aMCatNLO with heavy-quark coupling constraints. <a href="https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP08(2023)040" target="_blank">JHEP 2023 (2023) 040</a>.
