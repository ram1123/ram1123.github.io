Gave a plenary invited talk titled "Standard Model and Electroweak Results from CMS" at LISHEP 2021 (Workshop on Hadronic Interactions, Symmetries, and High Energy Physics). The talk summarised the latest CMS precision electroweak measurements and Standard Model results, covering vector boson production, VBS, and Higgs cross sections.
