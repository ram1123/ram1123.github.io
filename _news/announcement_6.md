Gave two invited talks in autumn 2022: a plenary talk on "H Effective Field Theories" at the HH2022 workshop in Paris, and a parallel talk on "Higgs pT measurements in ATLAS and CMS" at QCD@LHC 2022 in Durham. Both talks presented the latest CMS results on Higgs differential cross sections and EFT interpretations.
