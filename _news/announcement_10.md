Completed my Ph.D. in High Energy Physics at the University of Delhi. The thesis, titled <em>"Search For Anomalous Gauge Coupling through Vector Boson Scattering and Development of the GEM Detectors at the CMS Experiment,"</em> covered both a world-leading search for anomalous quartic gauge couplings in semileptonic diboson final states and hardware contributions to the CMS GE1/1 GEM muon upgrade.
