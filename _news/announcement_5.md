The CMS paper "Evidence for WW/WZ vector boson scattering in the decay channel lνqq produced in association with two jets" has been published in <em>Physics Letters B</em>. This result — establishing 4.4σ evidence for semileptonic vector boson scattering — is a milestone I contributed to as a key analyst on the analysis workflow and statistical interpretation. <a href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physletb.2022.137438" target="_blank">PLB 834 (2022) 137438</a>.
