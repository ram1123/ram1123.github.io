Gave a parallel talk titled "Searches for BSM scalars — CMS" at the Large Hadron Collider Physics (LHCP) 2023 conference in Belgrade. The talk covered CMS searches for beyond-Standard-Model scalar particles, including resonant Higgs pair production and high-mass scalar searches, highlighting the latest Run-2 results and their physics implications.
